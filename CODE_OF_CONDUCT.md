# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Glaux to be a project anyone can contribute to. Everyone participating —
maintainers, contributors, and people opening their first issue — is expected to treat
others with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be welcoming to newcomers; everyone was new once.
- Respect that maintainers may decline a change, and that "no" is a complete answer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and whenever
someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/glauxlabs/glaux/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the repository owner directly through their GitHub profile. Reports
are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they
judge inappropriate — and are obligated to explain moderation decisions when asked.
